Folkloom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Folkloom ("the app"), published by Sutheesh Apps, handles information.

1. Information we collect

Folkloom does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location. Your game progress (levels, scores, times, settings) is stored on your own device.

2. Advertising

The app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general usage and device information to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (hint packs and removal of banner ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

4. Backup

Android may back up your game data to your Google account through its automatic backup feature. You may also create a backup code inside the app; this code is text that only you control.

5. Children

Folkloom is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We do not keep your data on our servers.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this address.

8. Contact

Questions? Write to sutheeshapps@gmail.com.
